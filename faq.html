General
What exactly does Guardrail do?+
Guardrail is an automated financial auditing platform built specifically for Nigerian SMEs and freelancers. It takes raw transaction data from any source — CSV exports from your bank, Excel files from your accountant, or direct Xero sync — and runs it through three core engines: Data Cleaning & Audit (verifies every transaction, removes duplicates, enforces ∑ Debits = ∑ Credits), Expense Auditing (monitors spending categories in real-time against your defined limits), and Goal Tracking (projects your end-of-period financial position and alerts you when you're off track). The result is a FIRS-compliant audit trail that would take a human accountant days, delivered in under 14 seconds.

Who is Guardrail built for?+
Guardrail is designed for: Nigerian SMEs with 5–500 employees who need automated compliance without hiring a full-time CFO; freelancers and sole traders who want to stay FIRS-compliant without becoming accountants; finance teams at mid-size companies who want to replace manual spreadsheet reconciliation with real-time alerts; and accountants and CFO consultants who manage multiple client books and want audit trails generated automatically. If you process more than 50 transactions per month and care about where your money actually goes, Guardrail is for you.

Is Guardrail FIRS-compliant?+
Yes. Every audit report Guardrail generates is formatted to meet FIRS (Federal Inland Revenue Service) requirements, including: proper VAT schedule formatting (7.5% VAT under the Finance Act 2024 amendments), transaction categorisation aligned with FIRS schedule requirements, debit/credit balance verification, and complete audit trails with timestamps. For Pro and Business plan customers, reports are exported in a format accepted directly by FIRS e-filing portals. We consult with Nigerian tax practitioners on every major update to ensure ongoing compliance.

How long does an audit take?+
The average audit on Guardrail completes in 13.8 seconds for a standard 6-month transaction dataset (up to 2,000 entries). Larger datasets (up to 10,000 transactions) typically complete within 45–90 seconds. Real-time monitoring features like expense limit alerts and variance detection fire within 3 seconds of a new transaction being logged. For context: the same manual reconciliation by a trained accountant typically takes 4–12 hours for a comparable dataset.

Security & Privacy
How is my financial data protected?+
Your data is protected with 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit — the same standard used by Tier-1 banks. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 compliant. Key security architecture details: all data is stored in Nigeria-region AWS servers (Lagos edge node); we never sell or share your transaction data with third parties; data is deleted within 30 days of account termination on request; and each workspace is isolated in its own encrypted tenant with no cross-client data access.
Was this helpful?
Does Guardrail have access to my bank account?+
No. Guardrail does not have direct access to your bank account. All financial data enters Guardrail via CSV/Excel upload (exported from your bank's portal) or through your accounting software (Xero sync is read-only). We use OAuth 2.0 for third-party integrations, meaning we only receive the transaction data you explicitly authorise — never your login credentials. We cannot initiate transactions, move money, or access any data beyond what you import.
Was this helpful?
Can my team members see all financial data?+
You control exactly what each team member can see. Guardrail has three access levels: Admin (full access — ledger, reports, settings, billing); Editor (can upload, categorise, and run audits, but cannot change settings or view billing); Viewer (read-only access to dashboards and reports). You can also restrict access to specific ledger segments — for example, a team member can be given access to only the "Marketing" expense category without seeing payroll data.

Integrations
Which Nigerian banks' CSV formats does Guardrail support?+
Guardrail has native parsers for CSV/Excel exports from: GTBank, Zenith Bank, Access Bank, First Bank, UBA, Sterling Bank, Fidelity Bank, Stanbic IBTC, Polaris Bank, Keystone Bank, and all CBN-licensed fintechs (Opay, Moniepoint, Kuda, PalmPay, Carbon). If your bank's format is not listed, upload the file and our parser will auto-detect the column structure. If auto-detection fails (rare), you can manually map columns — we save the mapping so future imports from that bank are automatic.

Technical
What file formats can I import?+
Guardrail accepts: CSV (.csv), Excel (.xlsx, .xls), ODS (LibreOffice Calc), and PDF bank statements (via OCR — Pro plan and above, accuracy varies by bank formatting). Maximum file size is 50MB per upload. For datasets larger than 50MB, use our chunked upload feature (split by date range) or contact support for a direct import.

Is there an API I can use to automate imports?+
Yes. Pro plan includes read-only API access (pull audit reports, view ledger data, export to JSON/CSV). Business plan includes full read/write API access (push transactions programmatically, trigger audits, receive webhook alerts). API documentation is available at docs.guardrailfinancial.ng. Rate limits: Free — no API access; Pro — 1,000 requests/day; Business — 10,000 requests/day. Enterprise can request custom limits.
